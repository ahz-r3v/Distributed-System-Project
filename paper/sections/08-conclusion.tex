\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Across five replication settings, prefix/LRU has the lowest mean session serving time; predictive eviction helps FCFS but regresses under prefix-aware scheduling.
In the separate host study, adding 36~GiB of backing KV capacity yields reported $9.1\times$ and $4.8\times$ baseline-to-restoration time ratios at two loads.
Fetch-aware ordering further favors host hits, with promising but sparsely repeated observations.
The completed scope is adapted replication and host-DRAM recovery; SSD performance remains unevaluated.
