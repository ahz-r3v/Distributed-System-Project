\subsection{Research Questions and Intended Contributions}
This paper is organized around three research questions:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Which CacheWise findings can we reproduce under our experimental setup?
  \item Under what workload and hardware conditions does KV cache offloading cost less than prefix recomputation?
  \item How should a secondary-storage offloading mechanism exploit those conditions while controlling I/O overhead?
\end{enumerate}
The completed contribution is an adapted replication with an initial six-policy study and a separate 60-run coverage study.
It identifies a scheduler-dependent eviction effect across the tested loads and cohorts, reports full eviction-event counts, and distinguishes average session improvements from request-tail behavior.
The coverage study retains one observed preemption with a declared protocol deviation and an explicit limit on computation accounting.
